What was a doric?

“I dunno man. I think you both need protecting. I know you talk a big game about being at the Centaurs, but you’re a star player, man. You and Hollander, both. You deserve to be on teams that get your blood pumping and playing opponents that match your skill. But you don’t get that in Ottawa, and Shane doesn’t get that when he’s being fucked up by Montreal. This feels like something waiting to explode. For both of you. But I feel like… you already lost everything to be where you are.”

“Not everything.” Ilya had to interrupt. “Not even… Yes, Russia. Yes, Boston. Yes, my cups and my team and all the things that Boston meant are things I lost because I move teams for us. If I am here and I never go back — if I cut all contact — they are protected. Svetlana is crazy and says she doesn’t mind, but I worry about her too.”

“No. It’s good. I’m glad. You are good friend to both of us. It’s nice — I didn’t expect to have this. We have a plan that we wait until one or both of us retire. I had thought… I didn’t get to go out and hold his hand and kiss his cheek until then. So this is already much better than we imagined.”
