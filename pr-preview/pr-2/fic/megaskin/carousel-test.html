Instagram carousel test

Hi! Thanks for helping me test this.

So, here's the thing about carousels. Most of the carousel code out there uses things AO3 won't accept - scripts, animations, buttons - and AO3 quietly strips all of that out when you post, leaving you with a pile of photos and no way to move between them. Not ideal.

Both of the options below only use HTML and CSS that AO3 actually keeps, so either one could go in my Megaskin. I've tried both of them on AO3 itself and they work exactly like they do here. I just need to know which one works best for real people on real phones and computers.

Please have a play with both, in light and dark mode, and then tell me:

  • which option you like better
  • if anything looked broken, wobbly or just weird
  • what you were using (phone, tablet or computer, and which browser)

Option 1: Tap Arrows

This one uses the same trick as the like button on my Instagram posts. Tap the arrow on the right and the next photo slides in, and the arrow on the left slides it back out again. Tapping the photo itself shouldn't do anything.
