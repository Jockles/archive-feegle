“Hey, uh. Don’t say anything. About me being Jane. I’m, uh, not really ready to let this go actually public.”

“Thanks. I think. Now go, I don’t want you to have any excuses when we roast you tonight.”

“See you on the ice.”

“See you on the ice.” And then, Ryan was gone.
