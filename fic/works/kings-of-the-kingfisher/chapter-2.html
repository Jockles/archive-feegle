When Scott came back he was dragging a pair of chairs behind him. Following behind was Kip and Fabian, each laden down with as many drinks as they could carry, and behind them was Eric Bennett. He looked a little uncertain at the invitation, but he too was carrying drinks, with the bartender — Kyle? Ilya thought he recognised him — dragging another pair of chairs.

“If you manage to break a booth you either gotta pay for it, or you’ve gotta sign it. All of you.” Kyle helped Scott arrange the chairs to get everyone as close as possible, and then saluted. “Also don’t let Kip pick up the drinks, he keeps forgetting that he shouldn’t be behind the bar. And he’s crap at drawing pints when he’s drunk.”

“Thanks Kyle. We’ll do our best.”

With a nod, Kyle turned around back to the bar. Eric put the drink he was holding in front of Ilya, who’s eyebrows rode up in surprise.

“Kip said you weren’t drinking. Kyle made me a mocktail - thought you might want one too.”

Ilya was touched by the thought, and nodded his thanks.

When everyone was eventually sitting down, Ilya pushed his straw up and down in what he was pretty sure was a mojito. Sorry - a virgin mojito. That Eric Bennet had brought for him. Eric’s eyes now were trained back at the bar, where Kyle was gesticulating wildly while also pulling bottles out for various other Admirals. It was fairly impressive.

“So, uh, I’m guessing this was a surprise to everyone?” Vaughan said, gesturing between Ilya and Shane. Whatever bravado had powered Shane through his introduction had disappeared and Ilya got to watch his blush light up his face like a christmas tree.

“I knewwwww” Kip said in a sing-song.

“Yeah, we also knew.” Ryan gestured between himself and Fabian.

“I did not know. Well, I knew that Ilya had a guy. And I knew that he knew Hollander was gay before me.” Scott blew out a breath, “but this is, uh. Wow.”

“Sorry, you knew Hollander was gay?” Bennet asked.

“Oh, yeah. You remember that, uh, statement I put out? About captains supporting their teams? That was for Shane’s benefit.” Scott explained.

“And then he nearly got cancelled for being homophobic because the Voyageurs weren’t on your ‘Hunter Shortlist’.” Vaughan laughed.

“Shane, I am so sorry for that. Truly.” Scott reached across the table to try and put a Hand on Shane, but he was too far away and Shane had to reach out and shake his hand.

“Ha, uh. Yeah. Not gonna lie, made a very weird time a lot weirder. I couldn’t figure out if the coaching staff was more mad at me for not putting the team on the list or for making them feel bad about not being on the list. My mom thinks they were more mad about not being on the list because they don’t have a conscience, but she’s… really soured on the team recently.”

“Oh, strange. I wonder why that could be,” Ilya said, dripping with sarcasm.

“Yeah. Shane. That’s, uh. That’s really rough. Management are being weird?” Scott asked. “I mean… as much as I hate to say it, it being weird in the changing room is not totally unexpected. You’ll notice we’ve not got every Admiral here tonight, after all. Nothing as shitty as what’s going on in your room, don’t get me wrong, but there’s no rule that says we all have to be best friends. But like, the coaching staff and all that know that I could level a helluva lawsuit against them for open discrimination like that.”

“They know that I’d have to come out publicly to do that. And, well. I’m not ready. And they know that because I told them. I thought it would be fucking reassuring that I didn’t want to come out publicly. And I…” Ilya felt Shane’s hand tighten around his, and he increased the pressure of his own grip. He knew all this, they’d talked about all of this, but it still fucking killed him. “I’m still not ready. I want what I do on the ice to be about my hockey and not my… everything else. I get enough of the everything else following me out there already.”

“That’s fucking shit, man.” Said Vaughan after a beat of silence, and the rest of the table joined in, their collective words of disgust at the league and support of Shane washing over Ilya.

He could feel Shane starting to pull away, he looked at his face and saw how his eyes had started to glaze over in the way they did when he was starting to get overwhelmed by everything, and so he decided to pull the attention away from him.

“Okay, but did you see that guy on twitter who said he had a friend with a boyfriend on a professional team? If that is one of you guys I will be very disappointed because I am working very hard to have gayest team in league and I will not lose to fucking New York.”

“Fuck, imagine if all that shit about Crosby online was real. A fucking legend and he’s been fucking his teammate the whole time,” laughed Vaughan.

“I think if he was, he’d have way more awareness of the online nonsense. He thinks it’s like three rabid fans, not a whole community,” said Eric.

“Oh, you’ve asked him?”

“What happens at All-Stars, stays at All-Stars.”

Ilya let the rest of the table talk over each other. Kip and Fabian were both on their phones, rating players from other teams based on whether they gave off ‘vibes’, while Eric and Ryan critiqued their choices. Scott was describing the Game changer camp to Vaughan, who seemed to be actually serious about coming along to help out, and Miles was listening in as though he had any interest in the process.

Which left Ilya to take a moment with Shane.

“Shane, do you need to go or stay?” Shane nodded, and Ilya suppressed a groan. “That wasn’t a yes or no question, moi myatny listik.”

“…leaf?” Shane murmured.

“Mint leaf, yes. Like in my drink.” Ilya smiled. Making him think about Russian vocabulary was not a guaranteed way to snap him out of a spiral, but he couldn’t help but smile when Shane proves that his Russian was improving.

“I’m okay. Just need… quiet. I think.”

“We can find quieter spot? There are more people in here than space, it is very loud.” Ilya loved loud, loved feeling surrounded by people, but even he could admit that this room felt stuffy. And sometimes Shane needed to be told that he wasn’t being ‘overly’ sensitive, that he was feeling something that was happening to other people.

“No, this is fine. You make things quieter.”

“That is not what you usually say to me.”

“Oh shut up. You know what I mean.” Shane kissed Ilya gently on the cheek and Ilya leaned more heavily against him. Shane leaned in to the pressure, and Ilya left him to it, watching the rest of the table with interest.

After a while, Kyle came over and grabbed up their empties, and Scott offered to buy another round. Ilya tipped his now-empty drink toward Eric with a smile, and he ordered repeats for both of them. He watched the way Eric was staring at Kyle, how his eyes lingered on the bartender’s lips when he spoke, how he seemed to flinch from Kyle brushing against him gently as though he was hiding from something.

He also watched the way Vaughan was leaning very comfortably against Miles. He hadn’t even noticed, but the two of them had drifted together as though magnetic

Ilya got out his phone.

Ilya: I will put $500 that Eric Bennett and Carter Vaughan have boyfriends by the end of the season.

Ilya watched Scott and Ryan both pick up their phones. Ryan frowned, and Scott let out a squawk of laughter before slamming his phone down when Vaughan tried to read over his shoulder

Ryan showed Fabian who put a hand over his mouth, stared at the two men in question and then grinned.

Hockey Bear: Sucker bet. I’ll wager only one of them. Not sure which though.

Scott was trying to type while angling his phone away from Vaughan subtly, and it wasn’t working.

Old Man Scott: OH MY GOD Carter literally has a superstar girlfriend

Ilya: So did Shane.

“Ilya, you’re the worst.” Scott said aloud.

“You’re just mad that I’m so much more sexy and correct than you.” Ilya said in return.

“What the fuck are you talking about?” Vaughan asked, and Scott groaned, putting a hand over his face.

“Sorry, big gay secrets. You cannot know until you are also a big gay.”

Ilya: Problem is if I am right then I will have to work hard at making Centaurs more gay.

Ilya: cannot do it with my personal charm as I am taken.

Ilya: I will tell scouts to pick only gay rookies next season

Ilya: Either we are amazing team and win cup, or we get good draft pick and can choose best baby gays for team. Win-Win.

Hockey Bear: If I thought you had any influence over the Ottawa development selection I’d be more worried.

Old Man Scott: Is this the gay agenda that people keep telling me that I’m promoting? because I want to formally lodge a complaint.

Ilya saw Fabian take Ryan’s phone and start typing.

Hockey Bear: Ok but on a more serious note - you know there’s that LGBT hockey team in San Francisco, right? You guys should see if they want to do, I dunno, something.

Old Man Scott: What, like an LGBT Exhibition game?

Ilya: I think Max will want to join. Or be involved

Ilya scrolled his phone so that the messages about the bet weren’t visible and tilted his phone toward Shane. He read them in silence, and then murmured. “Well, might as well add me to the chat now,” which Ilya did.

[Canada is most gay]

Jane was added to the chat.

Jane: I don’t know that Max would want to join us specifically as he’s still pretty mad about not being allowed to join a pro league.

Ilya: We can ask.

Jane: And you’re forgetting something important

Jane: In order to make up a professional gay exhibition team you’d need at least five OUT gay people.

Jane: We have one.

Old Man Scott: You’re right he is a smart one

Ilya: I am always right.

Ilya could see Shane’s smile out of the corner of his eye and was pleased that he was clearly feeling better. He liked when Shane showed off, but usually he had to play up being annoyed by it around the rest of the hockey world. That had started to change over the last year since the foundation, but it was nice to just let Shane be himself, and bask in it.

His phone buzzed again in his hand.

Jane: Also nobody take Ilya’s bets on these things he’s like a bloodhound

Old Man Scott: A gay bloodhound?

Ilya: Is true I can smell gay people. Unfortunately hockey players smell very strong.

Jane: Ilya stop being gross

Ilya: no 👅🍑

Old Man Scott: If you two use this group to flirt I’m kicking Ilya out

Ilya made an exaggerated outraged face at Scott who just shrugged, then reached over to Shane again to get a high-five.

“Traitor,” Ilya muttered.

“Okay guys, you gotta stop texting when you’re right next to each other. Or at least tell us what you’re texting about.” Vaughan said, and went to grab Scott’s phone.

“It’s okay, Vaughan. You will know one day. For now you must wait in suspense.” Ilya smiled. “Maybe I will tell you during game and it will put you off .”

“Is it a hate crime if it’s gay-on-straight violence?”

“No, unfortunately. If anyone had the power to make Roz less….” Scott waved his hands vaguely toward him, “Roz, they would have by now.”

They were interrupted from anything further by the arrival of Kyle and a large collection of drinks, and then they were all distracted by the fact that Eric and Kyle were having the most awkward conversation that Ilya had ever seen, and he wasn’t entirely sure if Kip was helping or hindering by interjecting about Kyle’s interest in some very specific art that meant nothing to Ilya.

What was a doric?

While the table reshuffled to make space for Kyle to hang out with them, Shane started to shuffle his way out.

“Just need the bathroom. I’m fine,” was his response to Ilya’s worrying, and so he let Shane disappear around the corner to where the bathrooms were.

“Hey so, Roz.” Ilya turned back to the table and met Ryan’s eyes, which were unusually serious. His hands were fluttering nervously on the table, running up and down the condensation on his glass, but his face reminded Ilya of facing against him on the ice.

“Price,” Ilya said in response, his body tense.

“Look, I know we’re friends. And I know Hollander — he probably wouldn’t put up with your shit if he couldn’t give at least a bit of it back. But he’s scared. More scared than I was about hockey. More scared than I think anyone has been about coming out since it lost the death penalty. Fuck, I think if he lost hockey he’d feel like it was the death penalty.”

“You are not wrong. Every plan I make, everything I say… hockey is the one thing where he will never compromise. He hears words like Legend and Dynasty and they are not words that make him proud, they make him heavy. The Voyageurs do not love him as he loves them. But I think maybe they both love an idea, not a reality.”

“Yeah, you get it. And you also get that this means you have… you have all the power here, dude. You’re like most of the way to Canadian residency. I know that the decision to leave Russia was hard and, like, I see it now. What you gave up for him. But he’s not… don’t take it out on him, yeah?”

“I don’t know what you are trying to say. Are you giving me warning? You think Hollander needs protecting?”

“I dunno man. I think you both need protecting. I know you talk a big game about being at the Centaurs, but you’re a star player, man. You and Hollander, both. You deserve to be on teams that get your blood pumping and playing opponents that match your skill. But you don’t get that in Ottawa, and Shane doesn’t get that when he’s being fucked up by Montreal. This feels like something waiting to explode. For both of you. But I feel like… you already lost everything to be where you are.”

“Not everything.” Ilya had to interrupt. “Not even… Yes, Russia. Yes, Boston. Yes, my cups and my team and all the things that Boston meant are things I lost because I move teams for us. But I had lost Russia even without Shane. If I was okay hiding forever, being always with women and never even thinking about men then… maybe. But I had already done things that could get me in trouble before I ever set foot on this continent. I was already thinking about the danger all the time. And the danger has changed — got much worse — since I have been playing hockey. It is not just my danger but also for my family. My niece. My friends who still stay at home. If I am here and I never go back — if I cut all contact — they are protected. Svetlana is crazy and says she doesn’t mind, but I worry about her too.”

“Shit Roz, I’m sorry. I didn’t know.”

“I want you to worry about Shane. I worry about Shane. Sometimes I think Shane worries enough about Shane to change the whole MLH. I think he deserves good friends that think about him and keep him safe. I am very glad that he talks to Fabian and Kip about being gay. Being Russian in America means that I know many Russians that moved here for reasons not related to hockey. The Russian queer scene is more than you would think. So I have more… experience. Shane worries and it makes it hard for him to find experience — even experience that isn’t sleeping with other men. He is always thinking about optics and narrative and brand deals and being looked up to. These things are reasons why I love him, even when I hate that they make him feel even more heavy. He has a lot of heavy. So I try to be light.”

“Yeah man. That’s good. I’m glad. And, uh, I mostly promised Fabian I’d give you the shovel talk because he thinks Shane is funny and sweet and he only knows the version of you that gets interviewed after hockey games. I know you better, and I’m sorry I doubted you.”

“No. It’s good. I’m glad. You are good friend to both of us. It’s nice — I didn’t expect to have this. We have a plan that we wait until one or both of us retire. I had thought… I didn’t get to go out and hold his hand and kiss his cheek until then. So this is already much better than we imagined.”
