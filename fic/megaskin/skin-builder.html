Megaskin by Jockles

Basic Skin Builder

Tick the apps you want, paste in your profiles, and this puts together a work skin with only those apps and only your characters. The example profiles from the tutorial are taken out. The core is always in, because every app needs it.

Nothing you paste leaves your browser. When you're done, copy the skin or download it, and paste it into the CSS box of a work skin on AO3.

Don't need your own profiles yet? Here's the whole thing with no profiles in it: the full megaskin, and the core on its own.

New here? 'Tutorial' walks you through making a skin, step by step. 'Help text' shows a short explanation under every box. Without it, hover over or tap any ? to see the help for that box.

Apps Tick every app you use in your fic. You can come back and make the skin again with more apps later.

Loading the apps...

Your profiles Paste the CSS for your characters here: the profiles from the Profile Builder, or the CSS file the Fic Builder saves. Leave it empty for a skin with no profiles.

Your skin This is the whole skin, made from what you've picked. It changes as you go. Copy all of it, from the first /* to the last */.
