Megaskin by Jockles

Profile Maker

Fill in the boxes and the chats on the right update as you type, using the real megaskin. When you're happy, copy the CSS at the bottom and paste it into the PROFILES section of your work skin, next to the other profiles. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Only the first box is essential. Leave anything you don't need empty and it won't go in the CSS. Pictures need a direct link to the image file, usually ending in .jpg or .png, from somewhere like squidge.org or Tumblr.

The basics
pfp-

Small letters, numbers and hyphens only. It's filled in from the name until you change it.

Different on one app

Only fill in the ones that are different from the basics.

Extra accounts

A second account on one app - a private Instagram, a burner, a fan account. Give each one a name, then fill in only what's different from their main account. In your HTML, add the acc- name next to the profile, like pfp-stan acc-burner.

Their phone

The WhatsApp wallpaper for chats on their phone (add pov-stan to the chat container). Leave both empty for the normal background.
