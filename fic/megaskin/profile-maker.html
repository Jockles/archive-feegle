Megaskin by Jockles

Profile Maker

Fill in the boxes and the chats on the right update as you type, using the real megaskin. When you're happy, copy the CSS at the bottom and paste it into the PROFILES section of your work skin, next to the other profiles. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Only the first box is essential. Leave anything you don't need empty and it won't go in the CSS. Pictures need a direct link to the image file, usually ending in .jpg or .png, from somewhere like Imgur or Tumblr.
