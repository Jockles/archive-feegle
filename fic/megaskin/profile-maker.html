Megaskin by Jockles

Profile Maker

Fill in the boxes and the chats on the right update as you type, using the default megaskin. When you're happy, copy the CSS at the bottom and paste it into the PROFILES section of your work skin, next to the other profiles. Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Building chats too? The Fic Builder has this built in.

To edit a profile you've already made, paste its CSS into the Your CSS box at the bottom (one profile at a time). The boxes fill in from it, and you can carry on from there.

Only the first box is essential. Leave anything you don't need empty and it won't go in the CSS. Pictures need a direct link to the image file, usually ending in .jpg or .png, from somewhere like squidge.org or Tumblr.

New here? 'Tutorial' walks you through making a character's profile, step by step. 'Help text' shows a short explanation under every box. Without it, hover over or tap any ? to see the help for that box. You can have either one on, or both.

The basics
The character's name, the way you want it to show in the chats.
A short label for this profile. It's how the skin knows which character is which, so you'll add it to their messages in your HTML, like 'pfp-stan'. No spaces, and no two characters can have the same one.
pfp-

Small letters, numbers and hyphens only. It's filled in from the name until you change it.

Optional. A picture for their circle in every app. Upload the picture somewhere like squidge.org first, then copy the link to the picture itself (it usually ends in .jpg or .png) and paste it here.

The colour of their name in WhatsApp groups and on Discord. Pick one that's easy to read on both light and dark backgrounds.
Their username on apps like Twitter, Instagram and Tumblr. Leave off the @, it's added for you.
Their email address, for the email apps. It doesn't have to be real.
Different on one app Optional. Maybe they use a different picture on Instagram, or a different name on Discord. Fill in only the boxes that are different, on the app's row. Empty boxes use the basics.
