aleg kou expression SRD

nice to meet you (a greeting to a stranger, which does not require sharing names)

Gesture: RL · toward the other person · open RL extended at chest height, stopping short of contact, held for one beat, then lowered. The speaker does not touch their own centre first (no name is being offered)

Gesture variants: Contact version, only once the other person has answered with the same gesture: a light press of the hand to their forearm. Large-bodied speakers may extend the whole forearm. With no free limb, the head or body inclines toward them and holds.

Source: Jockles - SRD
Notes: SRD decision 30 Sep 2026. aleg "good" + kou "skin-contact, a friendly touch, a connection between people". An introduction to a stranger, deliberately without names, because many enslaved people keep a private or secret name that is not easily shared. Names are exchanged separately (ek masa nu ...; te masu nu?).

sorel numeral SRD

thirty; also a full circle of close kin, a household or clan-family

Source: Jockles - SRD
Notes: SRD decision 30 Sep 2026. sor "three" + -rel "tens", fused into one word and written with a single r (the one fixed exception to booklet Rule 1.4, which otherwise writes doubled letters). The main larger counting unit, tied to the three moons and the roughly thirty-day moon-count. Multiples are formed multiplier first (top-sorel 60, sor-sorel 90). Used for ages (thirty is parent-age; sixty is elder-age), days, and counting close kin (Tusken clans of 20 to 30). Everyday counting stays decimal.
