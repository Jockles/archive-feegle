Practice

Answers are at the end of the booklet.

A. Translate into Amatakka. (1) The food is expensive. (2) I am well. (3) Who is that? (4) The speeder is fast.

B. Conjugate. (1) gamuu, 2pl future. (2) gamuu, 1sg simple past. (3) gamuu, 3sg present continuous. (4) gamuu, 2sg present continuous.

C. Make the plural. (1) spida (2) riiv (3) dol (4) amu (5) kash

D. Add the clitic. (1) my speeder (2) from you (3) I know it (4) with us

E. Translate. (1) Mother gives me food. (2) I do not know. (3) Do you sit? (4) When do you come? (5) Three fast speeders. (6) Food is given by Mother. (7) I fly away.

F. Build the verb. (1) teach (2) know each other (3) be known (4) seat someone

G. Say it another way. (1) The mother who sits. (2) It is I who sit. (3) Sit, all of you!

H. Use masuu or mittuu. (1) I was well. (2) There is food here. (3) I have a speeder. (4) You are my freedom.

I. Habit and source. (1) I used to sit (as a habit). (2) I will keep sitting (as a habit). (3) Mother sits, and I saw it myself. (4) Mother sits, so I was told.
