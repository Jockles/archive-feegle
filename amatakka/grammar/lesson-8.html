Lesson 8. Saying no, and saying yes

Rule 8.1 [CANON + ESTABLISHED] The negative particle is kel (also kol, koa). Kai means "yes, agreed".

Rule 8.2 [NEW] Kel stands before the verb it negates. Berber negation also comes before the verb.

AmatakkaEnglish
Kel essena.I do not know.
Kel essenaker.I will not know.
Kel aleg spida.The speeder is not good.
Kel gam!Do not sit!

Rule 8.3 [NEW] Negative words are built with kel-: kel-mi "nobody", kel-ma "nothing", kel-meni "nowhere". The verb is still negated by kel: Kel esseni kel-ma. "He knows nothing."

Rule 8.4 [NEW] A yes or no answer repeats the verb: Essenuh? "Do you know?" gets Essena. "I know." or Kel essena. "I do not know." Kai and kel alone are also fine.
