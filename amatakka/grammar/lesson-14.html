Mattama ek.I am well.[CANON]
Mai mituh?How are you? (literally "how do you endure?"; reply Mita "I endure" or Mattama ek)[SRD]
Boraluplease (literally "hope, may it happen now")[SRD]
Dakkaluyou're welcome[CANON] (everyday reply). The formal reply for a serious favour is kel depu [SRD], literally "no chain", meaning no debt.
Paduu eksorry, forgive me (gesture: one tap on the speaker, then an open hand held toward the person apologised to)[SRD]
Kou?excuse me (to pass, or to ask for attention; gesture: two taps, the first on the other person, the second on the speaker)[SRD]
Aleg kounice to meet you (a greeting to a stranger, with no names exchanged; many enslaved people keep a private name)[SRD]
