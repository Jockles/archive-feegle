Amatakka

Amatakka is the language of the enslaved people of Tatooine. This is my working dictionary and grammar for it, as it is used in The Small Rain Down. It builds on the words fialleril, BLW and other fans made before me, and every word is marked with where it comes from.

It is a work in progress: the grammar is provisional, and rules marked NEW are proposals, not canon.
